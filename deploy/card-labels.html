🖨️ Card labels — 8 cards

Sized 82 × 50 mm, a little smaller than a standard RFID card so the edges don't curl up when you stick them on.

  1. Press Print (or ⌘P). Choose A4, scale 100% — not "fit to page", or the sizing goes out.
  2. Cut along the dashed lines.
  3. Stick one on each card, then teach that card in the game so the picture and the tag match.

Short on time tonight? Don't cut and paste — just print it, lay the sheet on the table, and put each card on its picture. Works exactly as well for a first play, and your kids can hand you "the one with the monkey face" without reading a word.
