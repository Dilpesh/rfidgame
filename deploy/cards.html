🖨️ Card labels — Jungle Rescue Patrol (15 cards)

Sized 82 × 50 mm, slightly under a standard RFID card so the edges don't lift. Fills 2 A4 pages at 2 per row.

  1. Press Print (⌘P). A4, scale 100% — not "fit to page".
  2. Cut along the dashed lines and stick one on each card.
  3. Open the game, teach each card, then run the card warm-up so the kids meet the deck.

Short on time? Print it and lay the sheet on the table — the kids put each card on its picture. Works just as well and needs no scissors.
