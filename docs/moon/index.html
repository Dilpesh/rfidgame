The Monkey Who Stole the Moon

How are you playing?

This game is made for physical RFID cards, but it plays perfectly well without them — handy for trying it out, or when the reader isn’t to hand.

With a reader you scan the real cards. On screen, the game offers you six cards each turn and the child taps one.

Teach the cards

Tap Teach all and scan each card as it lights up. The game remembers them on this device, so you only ever do this once.
