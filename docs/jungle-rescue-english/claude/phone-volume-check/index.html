Phone volume check

Jungle Rescue sets every background level with audio.volume. iPhones are said to ignore that. This page plays the same 15 s of the lion-scene bed three ways so you can hear whether yours does. Open it on the phone, speaker on, and press the buttons in order.

1 · Full level

The bed at volume 1.0. This is your reference.

2 · The game's way

The same bed at audio.volume = 0.26, exactly as index.html does it. It should be clearly quieter than step 1. If it sounds just as loud, the phone is ignoring the volume and every bed in the game is playing too loud on it.

3 · The fix

The same bed at 0.26 through a Web Audio gain node. This should be quiet on every phone.
