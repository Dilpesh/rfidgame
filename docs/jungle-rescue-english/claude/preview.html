2 · Lion beds — phone-speaker band test

Superseded: the tables below are for the first proposal (EQ'd crickets, B). The chosen E bed measures −20.6 dB (JR_103_bed @0.26) and −22.0 dB (JR_129_bed @0.18), both PASS; the files in modified/ are now E. See HOW_TO_ADOPT.md.

Gap = bed minus voice, through the phone simulation, bed at its in-game volume. Needs ≤ −20 dB in every band; standard targets −25 to −30.
