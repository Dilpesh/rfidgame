🖨️ Card labels — Toy Town Express (4 new cards)

Portrait, 50 × 80 mm. That sits inside a standard RFID card (54 × 85.6 mm) on every side, so nothing overhangs and no corner peels.

  1. Press Print (or ⌘P). Choose A4, scale 100% — not "fit to page", or the sizing goes out.
  2. Cut along the dashed lines.
  3. Stick one on each card, then scan that card and send me the UID so it works in the game.
The picture does the work. The kids can't read yet, so the drawing gets most of the card and the words sit at the bottom for you. Each one has a different silhouette — long and thin, round with a neck, a circle on a stand, a bitten disc — so they're told apart by shape first and colour second.

Water and Music already have labels in the Jungle Rescue and Moon sheets and already work in this game — you only need these four.
