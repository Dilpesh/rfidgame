Card Quest

Listen to a story, find the right card, save the day. Put the phone down — this one is played with your ears.

No card reader? Every game plays on screen too
🎴How it works. A voice tells the story and asks for a card. Your child finds it and taps it on the reader — or taps it right on the screen if you haven't got one. Six cards are offered each turn, and the one being asked for is always among them, so nobody ever gets stuck.
