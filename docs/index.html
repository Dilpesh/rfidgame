🎴 Card Games for Kids

Audio adventures played with physical cards — put the phone down, listen, and tap the right card. No reader? Every game plays on screen too.

🐆
Jungle Rescue Patrol
जंगल बचाओ टीम
Fuel the jeep and rescue five animals in trouble — an elephant stuck in a pit, a giraffe who can't reach the mangoes, a parrot with a hurt wing, and a lion afraid of the dark. Ends in a disco.

The Monkey Who Stole the Moon
चाँद चोर बंदर
The moon is missing and the jungle is dark. Jump a river, clap a parrot awake, creep past a snoring lion and laugh a sad monkey down from his tree. Winds down into bedtime on purpose.
8 cards~10 min HindiPlays without a reader

Find & Tap
A quick ears-only scavenger hunt: a voice asks for a colour or a shape, and the child finds the matching card. Short rounds, good for the youngest players and for warming up before a longer story.

No card reader? Pick “No reader — tap on screen” when a game asks. It offers six cards each turn and the child taps one — the right card is always among them, so the story can never get stuck.
