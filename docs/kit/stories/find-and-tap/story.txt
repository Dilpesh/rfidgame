# Find & Tap — ported from find-and-tap.src.html (the base64 build in docs/find-and-tap/).
# Same fifteen human-voice clips (audio_human/) and the same background loop; the game asks
# for the eight cards in a random order, praises with one of two lines, and ends with the outro.
# What changed on purpose: a card that does not come is asked for again at 8 / 17 / 28 s
# (the old game had a Replay button instead), and wrong-card lines rotate rather than pick at random.

title: Find & Tap
key: find-and-tap
language: en
minutes: 3
audio: audio/
cache: ft-1
hints at: 8s 17s 28s
tap sound: @lib:tap             # a soft tick when a card is tapped while Coco is still talking
voices: NARRATOR=luvvoice (human English)
activities: Look around the room for the card that is asked for

card RED: 🔴 Red
card BLUE: 🔵 Blue
card YELLOW: 🟡 Yellow
card GREEN: 🟢 Green
card CIRCLE: ⚪ Circle
card SQUARE: 🟦 Square
card TRIANGLE: 🔺 Triangle
card STAR: ⭐ Star


== block praise
one of:
  NARRATOR [delighted] @correct_1 #praise: Yay! You found it! Great job!
  NARRATOR [delighted] @correct_2 #praise: Wonderful! That's exactly right!
