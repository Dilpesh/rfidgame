🖨️ Card labels — Bobo Ki Birthday Party (9 cards)

The five Bobo cards, a blanket, and a spare Cake, Candle and Birthday Cap — one A4 page. A physical card means the same thing in every game, so it gets one label. Portrait, sized to a standard ID / RFID card (ISO 7810 ID-1, "CR80", 54 × 85.6 mm).


  1. Press Print (⌘P). A4, scale 100% — not "fit to page", or the sizing goes out. 9 cards per page, 3 pages.
  2. Cut along the black border. Stick one on each card.
Drawn, not emoji. Every picture is a bold outline drawing with flat colour, so it prints dark on any printer and looks the same on every phone. The words at the bottom are for the grown-up — the kids go by the picture.
