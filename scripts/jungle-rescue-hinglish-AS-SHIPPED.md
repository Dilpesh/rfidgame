# Jungle Rescue Patrol (Hinglish) — as shipped

`docs/jungle-rescue-hinglish` — **the build the kids actually played.**

This is a reconstruction, not a written script: this build never had one.
Its lines live inside the audio, and only 18 of them carry a caption in
the code. Where a line has no caption the text is genuinely unrecoverable
without listening — the cue, its order and its exact length are still
here, which is what the rewrite was measured against.

**85 audio assets · 10 stages · 10 cards**

## Why it was rewritten

Measured beat gaps — the longest stretch in each stage with nothing
landing (no effect, no short reaction):

| stage | longest gap |
|---|---|
| Rescue Jeep | 18s |
| Elephant rescue | 47s **— over the 30s rule** |
| A hungry friend | 25s |
| Giraffe rescue | 58s **— over the 30s rule** |
| A juicy treat | 22s |
| Parrot rescue | 30s |
| Water break | 34s **— over the 30s rule** |
| Lion rescue | 32s **— over the 30s rule** |
| Warm and cozy | 24s |
| Celebration | 44s **— over the 30s rule** |

Five of ten stages break the 30-second rule. The giraffe is the worst at
58 seconds, and the Celebration has **no sound effects at all** across 44
seconds — at the moment the child is meant to feel proudest.

See `scripts/jungle-rescue-v2-production.md` for the replacement.
