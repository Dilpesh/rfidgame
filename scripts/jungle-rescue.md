# Jungle Rescue Patrol — full script

**जंगल बचाओ टीम**

Captain Coco and the kids fuel the jeep and rescue five animals in trouble.
**18 card steps · 15 different cards · 3 min of narration**



> Hindi lines below are exactly what is recorded in the shipped game.
> Card cues show what the kids have to find. Play order is top to bottom.

---

## Opening

- 🗣 **`vo_intro_1.mp3`**  ·  14.7s
  - _direction:_ The first thing the kids hear. Say it with energy and stay close to the phone — the last take came out quieter than the rest of the story, which is why it needed rescuing. One clean run, no false start, nothing before or after it.

  > हैलो किशु मिशु! मैं हूँ कोको। आज हम जंगल बचाओ टीम बनेंगे! जंगल में हमारे कई दोस्त मुसीबत में हैं, और हमें उन्हें बचाना है!

- 🗣 **`vo_fuel_1.mp3`**  ·  6.9s
  - _direction:_ Ask for the jeep to be fuelled — three FUEL scans.

  > सबसे पहले हमें जीप में ईंधन भरना होगा। तीन बार फ्यूल कार्ड स्कैन करो!
