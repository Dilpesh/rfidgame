# Jungle Rescue Patrol v2 — full rewrite

Written against `STORY_CRAFT.md` after the first real session with both kids.
Nothing recorded yet.

**What changed and why:** the old story had two scenes that carried it — the
muddy pit and the elephant — and long flat stretches everywhere else, the
giraffe worst of all. This version is built so that **something lands every
20–30 seconds**: a joke you can picture, a sound effect, or appreciation of what
the child just did. Every scene below carries a beat clock, and no gap is over
30s.

## 3. Two things repeated on purpose

**"ये कौन करता है?!" was the strongest thing in either story.** Jungle Rescue
had no equivalent. It gets two, both used three or four times, always in the
same voice and rhythm, so the kids can join in.

**The rally — call and response.** Before every rescue:

> **Coco:** जंगल बचाओ टीम… **तैयार?!**
> *(one beat of silence — they shout back)*

**The mess cry.** Mess got the biggest laugh of the night, so it gets a name:

> **गंदा! गंदा! बहुत गंदा!**

Used exactly three times: the mud, the mango juice, and the frog.

**Every card ask rhymes**, so the instruction is the memorable line:

| card | ask |
|---|---|
| ROPE | रस्सी लाओ — **ताक़त लगाओ!** |
| SNACK | नाश्ता लाओ — **पेट भराओ!** |
| CLAP | ताली लाओ — **हिचकी भगाओ!** |
| LADDER | सीढ़ी लाओ — **ऊपर जाओ!** |
| MANGO | आम लाओ — **मुँह खिलाओ!** |
| TORCH | टॉर्च लाओ — **उजाला लाओ!** |
| MUSIC | म्यूज़िक लाओ — **सब नाचो!** |

**Acknowledgement, every single card, same shape:**

> **YES! <CARD>! शाबाश किशु मिशु!** + what changed in the world
